What I work on

3D molecular conformers

Geometry, not just connectivity. My models learn from three-dimensional atomic coordinates, which is what lets them tell one conformer of a molecule from another.

Current research · since March 2026

Multi-task learning for molecular property prediction

An independent research project on how multiple quantum-mechanical property tasks interact when a single model learns them together.

In brief

I build reproducible PyTorch pipelines on top of pretrained Uni-Mol representations and benchmark multi-task optimisation strategies across several data splits and seeds, with version-controlled experiment configurations throughout.

The work is ongoing. I'm glad to talk through the methods and findings directly — get in touch.
